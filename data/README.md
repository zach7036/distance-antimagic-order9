# Graph catalogues

Orders 1–7 are tiny graph6 catalogues retained directly in the repository. Orders 8 and 9 are downloaded on demand from Brendan McKay's ANU Combinatorial Data collection by `code/download_catalogues.py`; the downloader refuses the files unless their SHA-256 hashes match the completed study.
