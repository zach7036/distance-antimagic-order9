# Release 1.1.0 — August 13, 2026

This release expands the original order-nine project into a three-paper distance-antimagic research suite after a proof, computation, literature, and presentation audit.

## Included manuscripts

1. **Universal Distance-Antimagic Labelings: Cyclic Tests, Signed-Sum Intervals, and Complementary Neighborhoods**
2. **Nonlinear Group-Distance-Antimagic Labelings from Affine-Line Filters**
3. **The Distance-Antimagic Open-Neighborhood Conjecture Through Order Nine: An Exhaustive Certificate Verification**

## Major additions

- Exact universal cyclic/all-abelian classification: only complete graphs and perfect matchings survive every group labeling.
- Universal cyclic set-system classification via Johnson stars and tops.
- Exact signed-sum support theorem and complete cancellation/range/parity pair criterion for ordinary labels.
- Structural classifications for cluster graphs and forests, plus joined clique–matching and connected non-cograph families.
- Exact elementary-abelian-two pair theory and Seidel-switching classification of universal graphs.
- Corrected and generalized phantom-period/anti-period theory.
- Nonlinear affine-line finite-field constructions in every dimension at least three for infinitely many odd characteristics.
- Explicit finite-field certificates over `F_27`, `F_(7^4)`, and `F_(11^5)`.
- Complete order-nine certificate theorem retained with two independent verifiers.

## Important proof repairs

- The false unrestricted pair-collision lemma was replaced by the exact specialization of Nagy's cyclic permutational-sum theorem.
- The cyclic complementary-neighborhood exception is retained at pair level and eliminated globally by a Hamming-distance identity.
- The positive-integer converse is supplied by an exact signed-sum support theorem rather than computational evidence.
- The phantom-period implication was corrected from `2k=0` to `S+2k=S`; involutivity is recovered under aperiodicity.
- Affine-line set identities now include the required disjointness/zero-avoidance hypotheses.

## Verification snapshot

The archived regression suite checks signed-sum supports and constructive witnesses, universal graph criteria, structural integer families, elementary-abelian-two theory, anti-period and phantom-period families, finite-field certificates, exponent/gcd identities, and odd-dimensional root-of-unity constructions. The original order-nine archive independently verifies all 205,914 positive certificates among all 274,668 unlabeled order-nine graphs.

## Scope warning

These manuscripts do not solve the Kamatchi–Arumugam conjecture in general. The arbitrary fixed noncyclic-group pair problem for groups of exponent greater than two remains open. The work is not yet peer reviewed, and publication priority remains provisional pending specialist/database review.
